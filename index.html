Vision

Vision

To be a globally recognized institution for excellence in technical education and research, creating professionals who are technologically competent and ethically sound to address societal challenges.

Mission

Mission
  • To impart quality technical education through innovative teaching methods and research.
  • To foster a culture of innovation, entrepreneurship, and collaboration.
  • To prepare students to be lifelong learners and leaders in their chosen fields.
  • To provide a conducive environment for holistic development, integrating academic rigor with co-curricular activities.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  1. Apply knowledge and competence in the design and development of computer-based systems.
  2. Adapt to emerging technological advancements including cybersecurity and data science.
  3. Demonstrate professional ethics, effective communication skills, and a commitment to lifelong learning.
  4. Engage in teamwork, problem-solving, and critical thinking to solve complex engineering problems.
